## 3. Positioning

### 3.1 What Silver Tide Tech is
- A **product-led** company: it builds and operates its own products and platforms.
- A **technology and innovation** company: products, digital platforms and solutions for real-world problems, across more than one industry over time.
- A **parent brand**: businesses, products and ventures sit under it and share its standards.

### 3.2 What Silver Tide Tech is not (positioning guardrails)
- Not an "edtech company". Education is the first vertical, not the identity.
- Not an agency first. Technology services are a *planned* vertical alongside the products.
- Not a holding company with legal subsidiaries. The website uses **business, vertical, product and venture** terminology and makes no claims about legal entities or registrations.

### 3.3 How the tagline works
- **Building Technology** → what we do (products, platforms, solutions).
- **Empowering People** → why we do it (teachers today; students, professionals and businesses as the portfolio grows).

The name adds a quiet supporting idea: *a rising tide lifts all boats*. Progress that lifts the people around it. It is expressed visually through the tide-line motif (see brand direction), not as a second slogan.

## 7. Brand messaging

### 7.1 Messaging pillars

| Pillar | Message | What supports it today |
|---|---|---|
| **Product-led** | We build and run our own technology products. | SarasTech is live with seven capabilities |
| **People-first technology** | Technology should empower people and simplify their work. | SarasTech assists teachers with daily work |
| **Practical innovation** | Innovation that is accessible, practical and impactful. | The purpose statement; SarasTech's multilingual support |
| **Built for the long term** | A parent company designed to grow into new products, businesses and industries. | Education roadmap; planned services vertical |

### 7.2 Elevator statements

- **One line:** Silver Tide Tech: Building Technology. Empowering People.
- **Short (≈30 words):** Silver Tide Tech is a product-led technology and innovation company. We build technology products, digital platforms and solutions to solve real-world problems, starting with SarasTech, an AI Teaching Assistant for teachers.
- **Long (≈80 words):** Silver Tide Tech is a product-led technology and innovation company. We build technology products, digital platforms and solutions that empower people, simplify work and create long-term value. Education is our first major vertical. Our product SarasTech is an AI Teaching Assistant that helps teachers with lesson plans, question papers, quizzes, worksheets and classroom coaching, in multiple languages. We plan to expand into student learning, human-led online teaching and physical learning centres, and to offer technology and consulting services.

### 7.3 Claims policy
- No statistics, user numbers, customer logos, testimonials, awards or case studies until real and verifiable.
- Planned items always use future-intent language ("we plan to", "on our roadmap").
- No claims about legal structure, registrations, funding or partnerships unless the founders supply them.
